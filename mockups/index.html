OpenScout · UX/UI exploration

Five redesign directions

Same scouting hub, five quiet skins. Every direction shares one rethink: a single labeled nav, the whole live event visible at once (rankings, on-field, skills, scout panel) instead of eight buried tabs, a readable rankings table, and an always-present team scout panel.
